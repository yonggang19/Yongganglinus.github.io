About

Hi, I’m Yonggang. I am a Ph.D. candidate in Information Systems at the National University of Singapore (NUS), supervised by Provost’s Chair Professor Jungpil Hahn. I expect to complete my Ph.D. in November 2026.

My research examines artificial intelligence and organizational learning, human–AI collaboration, and the adaptation of complex systems. I use computational modeling, simulation, and machine learning to study how AI shapes learning and knowledge work in organizations.

My dissertation is titled Effect of Artificial Intelligence on Organizational Learning.

I am seeking academic positions. Please feel free to contact me about job opportunities or research collaborations.
